# Chat History Manager Privacy Policy

Chat History Manager helps you select, archive, and delete conversations in your ChatGPT history.

Data the extension uses. The extension reads conversation titles and links from your chat history. It uses them to identify chats, show your selection before you confirm an action, and check the archived chats list after an archive operation.

How actions are processed. The extension processes your selection in your browser. When you confirm an action, it uses ChatGPT’s controls to archive or delete the selected conversations in your account. When deleting a chat, the extension leaves the site’s file deletion setting as you configured it.

Data sharing. The developer does not receive your conversation titles, links, or messages. The extension does not send them to analytics or advertising services, sell them, or use them for credit decisions. Its use of user data is limited to managing the conversations you select, in accordance with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
